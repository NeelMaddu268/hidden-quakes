### REQ-H2-1 · Borehole sensor elevations: StationXML elevation is sometimes already the sensor elevation
- From: H2 (reviewer on MATCH-01)
- Needed by: before `stations.parquet` is published (it feeds the synthetic depth test and every location)
- What: for at least one borehole station near the cluster (FORK, channels EH*/GH*), StationXML station and channel Elevation (about 1409 m) already equals the sensor elevation. Channel Depth is 281 m, and the ground there is about 1686.5 m (USGS 3DEP 1 m via EPQS). Its EN*/GN* channels report the ground elevation instead. Applying docs/01's `sensorElevM = surfaceElevM - sensorDepthM` blindly to the EH*/GH* channels would put those sensors about 280 m too deep. Other boreholes (FORK2, FORK3, FSB*) report the ground and look consistent.
  1. For every borehole channel, check `Elevation - Depth` against the DEM ground and against sibling channels. Take `surfaceElevM` from the ground where StationXML already reports the sensor elevation, and log the correction.
  2. Log `surfaceElevM`, `sensorDepthM` and `sensorElevM` per station. H2 uses this as depth diagnostic row 1.
  3. Add a test pinning that station's sensor elevation near the ground minus the depth (about 1405 m), not about 1127 m.
  4. Say which vertical datum the StationXML elevations use. NAVD88 (what 3DEP and run.yaml use) and the ellipsoid differ by about 21 m here.
- Why it blocks: the station nearest the cluster sets the depth constraint. A few hundred metres of receiver-elevation error biases every depth and the synthetic test that licenses the "resolves depth to ±N m" claim.
- Status: done (feat/signal, SEIS-01; merges 5be7fea and 032f9a8)
  1. Every chosen triplet is checked against the USGS 3DEP DEM (EPQS) at the sensor position. The StationXML station elevation is classified as either surface or sensor level (`stations.elevation` in signal.yaml), and ambiguous cases fail loudly. `sensorElevM = surfaceElevM − sensorDepthM` always holds. Four stations turned out to be sensor-level: FORK, plus FSB4, FSB5 and FSB6. For the FSB stations the channel elevation is the ground but the station elevation is the sensor. For those four, `surfaceElevM = stationElev + depth`. One shallow station whose StationXML elevation misses the DEM by more than 50 m takes the DEM surface, and is flagged.
  2. Every decision, with station elevation, channel elevation, depth, DEM, surface, sensor and convention, is logged and written to `runs/<id>/inventory_report.json` → `stations[].elevation`.
  3. `tests/signal/test_inventory.py::test_fork_borehole_geophone_with_sensor_level_station_elevation` pins FORK's `sensorElevM` at the StationXML sensor level (about 1408 m), not about 1127 m. FSB4 is pinned too.
  4. Datum: I measured it rather than assumed it. Across 25 stations, StationXML elevation (or elevation + depth for the sensor-level ones) minus the 3DEP DEM has a median of 0.0 m and a mean of 0.1 m, with outliers from steep terrain. Ellipsoidal heights would sit about 21 m below the DEM. So the StationXML elevations here are orthometric, the same datum as 3DEP and run.yaml (NAVD88).
  Also found: one station's metadata said 200 Hz but EarthScope serves 100 Hz. Inventory now probes the served rate (`stations.rateCheck`), so its `sampleRateHz` and `preprocessProfile` follow the data.
